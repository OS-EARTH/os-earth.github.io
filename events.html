OS-Earth Geoscience Roundtable: Call for Participation

We are organizing a virtual roundtable to bring together researchers, developers, and practitioners from the geoscience and computing communities. The discussion will focus on key challenges in Earth-science research, including data access, data use, and data sharing, as well as software and computing infrastructure needs for geospatial analysis.
